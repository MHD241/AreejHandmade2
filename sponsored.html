What NUVOTAP does

A card that opens the right page.

NUVOTAP sells contactless NFC cards. Hold a compatible phone near the card and a link opens: a Google review page, a website, or a digital profile. There is no app to install and nothing to scan.

A tap never submits a review by itself. It simply puts the right page in front of the customer, who then chooses what to write. That keeps reviews genuine, and makes it easy for happy customers to say so.

How it works

Three steps, one tap.

01

Tap the card

Hold a compatible phone near the card at the till, reception desk or counter.

02

The page opens

The card opens your Google review page, website or digital profile straight away.

03

They decide

The customer chooses whether to leave a review or get in touch. Nothing is sent automatically.
